# Luminaria 0.2.8 (in progress — do not publish yet)

## Appearance
- New default look: black text on white. Dark mode is the reverse: white text on black.
- Choose Light, Dark or Follow system in **Settings & updates → Appearance**.
- Optional accent color for buttons, selection, focus and progress, picked freely.
  You can give dark mode its own accent.
- Every color can be changed, separately for light and dark mode, under
  **All … mode colors**. Low-contrast choices are flagged.
- Your look loads instantly when the app opens, without a flash of the wrong colors.
- If you had chosen dark mode before, it stays dark.

## Behind the scenes
- Luminaria now keeps a structured record of each task's work: model responses, tool
  calls and results, approvals and compaction. It is the foundation for the upcoming
  spatial workspace view.
- The model can share a plan for multi-step tasks. Plans are shown as the model's
  stated plan, separate from the actions that actually ran.
