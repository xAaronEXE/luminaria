# Luminaria 0.2.58

**Smarter about how it works.** Luminaria now adapts to what you ask:
- **Quick questions:** answered directly.
- **Current facts and unfamiliar topics:** researched, with sources cited.
- **Writing:** finished and polished.
- **Code:** read, reproduced, fixed at the root and tested, with small, focused changes (Ponytail's style).
- **Documents and computer tasks:** done with real files and checked.
- **Big jobs:** planned and worked through step by step.

It asks up to three short questions only when your request is truly unclear. When something fails, it works like an expert debugger: it reads the error, finds the cause, looks it up if needed and tries a different fix. After three different attempts, it stops and explains what it tried and your options. It always checks the work before saying it's done.

**Knows itself.** Luminaria now has its own manual built in, and knows your current settings: permission mode, models, Auto mode and more. It reads the manual when you ask how something in the app works, instead of guessing.

**Planning uses your smartest model.** Plan only now automatically uses your strongest installed reasoning model, with thinking on. Luminaria loads it for the plan and unloads it afterwards. You can choose a different one under Settings › Auto mode › Planning model.

**Research by default.** A new research tool searches several phrasings, reads the best pages from different sites, and brings back the relevant passages with their sources. Luminaria uses it first for plans, comparisons, current facts and anything unfamiliar, and cites what it used.

**Faster, better-tuned models.** Luminaria now runs your models with better settings:
- **Faster prompt reading.** Long chats and big tool results are read much faster.
- **Thread count matched to your PC.** Threads now match your processor's core count instead of a fixed number.
- **Recent chats kept in system memory.** Switching back to a chat doesn't make the model re-read it.
- **More room to think.** Reasoning models get more room to think before answering.

The Windows installer is still unsigned, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.
