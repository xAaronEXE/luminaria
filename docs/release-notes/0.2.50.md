# Luminaria 0.2.50

**Cleaner replies.**
- Each reply is now one message. The steps the assistant took and its answer appear together instead of as separate messages.
- Steps are summarized in plain English, such as "Listed files in your working folder" or "Ran 2 commands and read 3 files". Click to see each step, and click a step to see its full details.
- Steps that need you stand out: anything waiting for your approval opens by itself, and failed steps are marked.

**Fix.** The assistant no longer repeats its answer when it ends a reply with something like "let me know if you need anything else".

The Windows installer is still unsigned, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.
