# Luminaria 0.3.0

Luminaria's biggest update yet. It's smarter about your requests, does the work itself and handles obstacles. It also carries out long tasks step by step with the right model for each job, can use online model services when you allow it, and lets your PCs work together.

## Smarter about how it works
- **It adapts to what you ask.** Quick questions get direct answers. Current facts and unfamiliar topics are researched, with sources cited. Writing comes out finished and polished. Code is read, reproduced, fixed at the root and tested, with small, focused changes. Documents and computer tasks are done with real files and checked. Big jobs are planned and worked through step by step.
- **It asks only when it must.** Up to three short questions, and only when your request is truly unclear. Otherwise it states its assumption and carries on.
- **It handles obstacles.** When something fails, it reads the error, finds the cause, looks it up if needed and tries a different fix. After three different attempts, it stops and explains what it tried and your options.
- **It checks before saying it's done**, and tells you briefly what it did, where the results are and anything it didn't verify.
- **It does the work instead of handing it to you.** If it starts to paste a whole program into the chat, or tells you to open a terminal and install things, it saves the file, installs what's needed, runs it and fixes errors itself.
- **It knows itself.** Luminaria has its own manual built in and knows your current settings and installed models. It never makes up model names. Programs it writes can use your installed models through Luminaria's own local connection.

## Long tasks, step by step
- **Carry out a plan one step at a time.** In the plan card, **Carry out plan** now runs each step with a clean slate: just the plan, notes from earlier steps and that step's own work. Big jobs stay focused and use fewer tokens.
- **The right model for each step.** Coding steps go to your coding model, other steps to your chat model, and when everything is done your planning model checks the finished work against the goal. Luminaria loads and swaps models as it goes.
- **Model roles you can see.** The model picker shows which model *plans and checks* and which one *codes plan steps*. To change them, go to the new Settings › Model roles page.

## Model services and Relay
- **Add a model service** in Settings › Model services: OpenRouter, Groq, Gemini, OmniRoute, Ollama, LM Studio, or any service that works like OpenAI's API. You enter an address and a key, and the key is encrypted on your PC.
- **Use them anywhere.** Their models appear in the model picker under *Model services*.
- **Relay.** Set a model service as your Coding model and Luminaria relays the coding steps of a plan to it, while your own PC plans the work and checks the result.
- **You decide what leaves your PC.** Each service has a **May see my files** switch, off by default. When it's off, the service only gets the conversation text: no file contents, no command output, no file tools, and not your memory. Auto never sends anything to a service on its own.

## Your PCs, working together
- **Pair your PCs with a code.** On one PC, open Settings › Paired PCs and turn on **Let other PCs pair**. It shows a 6-digit code. On the other PC, choose **Pair with another PC**: Luminaria finds it on your network, and you type the code. That's it.
- **They work together.** A paired PC's models join yours. Auto mode and the model roles pick the best model across all your PCs, so one PC can plan while another codes, passing the work back and forth step by step. Your files stay on the PC you're using; the paired PC only runs the model.
- **Split big models.** Turn on **Split big models across paired PCs** to run one model on all your graphics cards at once, using llama.cpp's own RPC system. The paired PCs lend their graphics cards automatically. Use it on your own home or office network only.

## Settings, reorganized
- **New pages** under Settings › Models & voice: **Model roles**, **Model services** and **Paired PCs**, each with everything in one place.
- **Color profiles everywhere.** The new pages use your color profile (Aurora, Ember, Ocean, Forest or Mono) in both light and dark mode. Fit ratings and other colored labels are now darker in light mode, so they're easier to read on white.
- **The Model library page** now has the same name in its title as in the menu.
- **Task overview removed.** The 3D task overview view is gone. Progress is shown in the chat, the plan card and the Activity panel.

## Planning and research
- **Planning uses your smartest model.** Plan only uses your strongest installed reasoning model, with thinking on. It loads for the plan and unloads afterwards. To choose another, go to Settings › Model roles.
- **Planning is a conversation.** When you brainstorm or correct it, it replies with short notes and questions instead of printing the same plan again. The plan lives in its own card.
- **Research by default.** A new research tool searches several phrasings, reads the best pages from different sites, and brings back the relevant passages with their sources.
- **`/skill name` loads the skill** before Luminaria answers, so it follows the skill's steps instead of describing them.

## Sturdier
- **No more runaway replies.** If a model starts repeating itself, Luminaria stops it, keeps one copy and tells you. It also uses llama.cpp's standard anti-repetition setting.
- **Long chats keep working.** Very long old replies are shortened in Luminaria's working memory, and the full text is still saved. If summarizing a long chat fails, it carries on from your latest messages instead of stopping.
- **Installing packages works.** `pip install` installs into the same Python Luminaria runs your code with. Long installs can take up to 10 minutes. A missing module comes with the exact install command, for example `cv2` → `opencv-python`.
- **Clearer errors.** Commands that run too long say so plainly, instead of failing with a blank error.

## Models
- **Idle means idle.** When work is done, Luminaria stops completely and unloads the model from your graphics card after a short moment. To keep it loaded for 5 or 30 minutes, change Settings › Hardware & tuning › After it finishes.
- **Clear model status** in the bottom corner: *Model off*, *Model loaded · unloads in 0:15* or *Model working*.
- **The background and orb stay still while idle**, and "Working" only shows while a task is really running.
- **Faster and better tuned.** Prompts are read much faster. Thread count matches your processor. Recent chats are kept in system memory, so returning to one is quick. Reasoning models get more room to think.
- **New downloads work right away**, without restarting the app.
- **Fixed:** "No model is installed yet" appeared after you removed all your models and downloaded new ones.
- **AI scores in the Model library.** Each installed model can get an AI score out of 100 for its overall ability: 36 questions across reasoning, math, coding, knowledge, following instructions and tool use, graded automatically on your PC. **Score my models** scores them all, **Best AI score** sorts by it, and Auto mode takes it into account.
- **Select several models at once.** **Select models** lets you pick models and download or remove them together.
- **Tiny models are labelled.** Models under 7B are marked "Chat only: too small for tasks" in the model picker.

## Leaner behind the scenes
What Luminaria passes to the model is now compact plain text instead of JSON. Results from files, commands and the web use about 30% fewer tokens and reach the model exactly as written, which leaves more room for your conversation.

The Windows installer is still unsigned, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.
