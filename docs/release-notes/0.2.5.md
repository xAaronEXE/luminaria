- Models settings is now a model library. Standard and Abliterated models are listed separately, with an Installed filter.
- Download, cancel, resume, use, benchmark and remove models from one place. Downloads are checked against the publisher's SHA256 and stay inside Luminaria's models folder.
- A storage meter shows the model drive's capacity, used and free space, and how much space a download will leave.
- Updates now check Luminaria's own release service. Installers are verified before they open and are not code-signed yet.
