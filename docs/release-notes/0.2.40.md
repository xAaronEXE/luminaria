# Luminaria 0.2.40

Settings that are quicker to read and easier to change.

**Compact settings.** Every Settings page now has the same tidy layout. Related options sit together in a card, with the name and a short hint on the left and the control on the right. Each page has one Save button at the bottom. Longer explanations, like how Auto chooses a model or what compaction keeps, are folded away until you open them.

**Clearer menu.**
- Settings has four short groups: Account, Workspace, Models & voice, and App.
- Sign-in methods and Devices are now one page, **Sign-in & devices**.
- Orchestration is now called **Auto mode**.
- Tools & permissions is now called **Permissions**.

**Auto mode.**
- Choose which models Auto may use with one switcher: All, Standard, Abliterated, or Pick.
- One list shows every installed model and whether Auto can choose it, with the reason when it can't.
- Checkboxes appear only when you pick models yourself.

**Fixes.**
- The Library menu no longer hides behind the chat, and it closes when you click away or press Escape.
- The top bar gives the task title more room. Task overview shrinks to an icon on narrower windows.
- Messages fade at the top and bottom instead of being cut off.
- The options button on sidebar tasks shows when you point at a task, and the full title appears as a tooltip.
- Switches no longer show the label "On" or "Required" while they are off.
